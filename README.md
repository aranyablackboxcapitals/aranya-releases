## 🤔 What is Aranya?
Investing in Indian stocks shouldn't require staring at charts all day or second-guessing every decision. **Aranya** is a mobile app that removes the guesswork from stock trading by giving you a clear, rule-based system:
- **What to buy** — ranked opportunities based on real market data
- **When to buy more** — smart dip-averaging alerts when your stocks fall
- **When to sell** — profit targets that account for taxes and brokerage
You stay in control. Aranya doesn't auto-trade — it **guides your decisions** so you trade with confidence, not emotion.
---
## 🎯 How It Works
### Step 1: Set Up Your Strategy
Create a strategy with your capital amount. Aranya calculates everything around your budget — how many stocks to hold, how much to invest per stock, and your profit targets.
### Step 2: Follow the Signals
| Signal          | What It Means                                                                          |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🟢 **Fresh Buy** | A stock is near its 52-week low and showing recovery — good time to enter              |
| 🔵 **Dip Buy**   | A stock you own has dropped further — Aranya tells you exactly how much to add         |
| 🟡 **Sell**      | Your stock has hit its profit target (after accounting for taxes) — time to book gains |
### Step 3: Watch Your Portfolio Grow
Track your holdings in real-time with live market prices, and review your performance over time.

## 📱 What You Get
### 📊 Dashboard
Your command center — see your total net worth, today's profit/loss, cash available, and how many buy opportunities are waiting, all in one glance.
### 💡 Opportunities
Two tabs of actionable signals:
- **Fresh** — New stocks ranked by potential, with rising momentum filter
- **Dip** — Your existing stocks that have dipped enough to add more
### 💼 Holdings
Your live portfolio with real-time prices. Every stock shows:
- Current value and unrealized profit/loss
- Today's change (amount + percentage)
- Sell signal when profit target is reached
### 📈 Performance *(Pro)*
Understand how your strategy is performing:
- Return on investment (ROI) and annualized returns (XIRR)
- Win rate — what percentage of your trades are profitable
- Equity curve — your net worth over time
- Monthly P&L breakdown
### 📒 Trade Logs
A complete record of everything:
- **Cycles** — Full buy-to-sell journeys for each stock
- **Tradebook** — Every individual transaction
- **Ledger** — All deposits, withdrawals, and trades
### ⚙️ Settings
Customize your strategy rules:
- How many stocks to hold at once
- How much to invest per stock
- How aggressively to average down on dips
- Daily buy limits to control your pace

## ❓ FAQ
**Does Aranya place orders for me?**
No. Aranya tells you what to do — you execute the trades yourself on your broker's app (Zerodha, Groww, etc.).
**Is my data safe?**
Yes. Your data is encrypted, stored securely, and only accessible to you. We use bank-grade security with row-level data isolation.
**What market does it support?**
Aranya currently supports **NSE (National Stock Exchange)** listed stocks on the Indian market.
**Do I need to watch the app all day?**
No. Check the app once or twice during market hours. Aranya calculates everything for you — just follow the signals when they appear.
**What's the minimum capital?**
₹1,00,000 to create a strategy. This ensures the system has enough room to diversify and average down effectively.
